## 2. Course Description

> This course provides an in-depth introduction to neural networks and deep learning.
> Students will learn key methods for improving neural networks, including hyperparameter
> tuning, regularization, and optimization. Core topics include Convolutional Neural Networks
> (CNNs) for image tasks, sequence models for time-series and language, and generative models
> for data creation. Advanced topics cover recent developments in deep learning, including
> attention mechanisms, transformers, Large Language Models (LLMs), transfer learning, and
> ethical considerations. Through hands-on laboratories and projects, students will develop
> practical skills to build, optimize, evaluate, and apply deep learning models to real-world
> AI applications.

## 7. Topics Covered

1. Introduction to deep learning and its applications
2. The Backpropagation Algorithm
3. Training deep neural networks
4. Regularization and generalization
5. Optimization and hyperparameter tuning
6. Convolutional Neural Networks I — Foundations
7. Convolutional Neural Networks II — Architectures and transfer learning
8. Recurrent Neural Networks I — Modeling sequences
9. Recurrent Neural Networks II — LSTM and GRU
10. Attention mechanisms
11. Transformers
12. Large Language Models and transfer learning
13. Generative deep learning and GANs

### ⚠ OPEN — Ministry of Education weighting discrepancy

The instruction block directly above this table in the syllabus states:

> *"The grade distribution should follow the Ministry of Education bylaws, whereby the
> Midterm exam counts for 20% and the Final Exam (Practical and theoretical) counts for 40%
> for all courses except Research-based courses or capstone courses. Any deviation from this
> grading system should be discussed and approved in DC and CC and endorsed by the provost."*

The table lists the **Final Exam at 20%, not 40%**. CS 4085 is neither research-based nor a
capstone course. The weights do total 100%, so this is not an arithmetic error — it is either
(a) an approved deviation, or (b) an error in the table.

**Resolution required before:** exam blueprints, assessment calendar finalization, and CLO
attainment calculations. **Not blocking:** lecture and lab development.

**Until resolved, all workspace material uses the table as written (Final = 20%).**

## 15. Other Policies

### Class Participation
Students are strongly encouraged to ask and answer questions during lectures, contribute to
discussions in class and on the Discussion Board, respond constructively to classmates, and
engage in problem-solving and collaborative exercises.

### Student Development & Participation (5% of grade)
Fulfilled through the Deanship of Student Affairs **Ambassador Program** and Student Life
initiatives. Students refer to Student Affairs announcements for requirements.

### Generative AI Usage
Permitted **only if approved by the faculty**. When approved:
* Do not violate intellectual property laws.
* Do not include misinformation or unethical content.
* Clearly distinguish own work from AI-generated material.
* **AI-generated content must not exceed 25% of any submitted work.**
* Always provide proper citation. Failure to cite is plagiarism under the Effat Code of
  Conduct and Tarbawyyat guidelines.

### Academic Integrity
Governed by **Tarbawyyat Effat** and its eight pillars: Ţaqwã (piety), Ādab (knowledge,
manners, outreach), Ri'ayya (nurturing), Bunyan Marsūs (cooperation), Wasaţya (tolerance and
moderation), Yusr (modesty and ease), Amanah (ethics and integrity), Tawğyh (guidance).
Cheating, plagiarism, and taking unfair advantage are strictly prohibited. Violation
procedures: **Article 29** of the Effat University Bylaws for Undergraduate Study and
Examination and Implementation Rules.

### Faculty Availability
Email response within **up to 24 hours**. Office phone available during office hours.

### Course Resources
All materials (syllabus, lectures, assessments, activities, rubrics) available on Blackboard
from day one. All announcements posted to Blackboard. All submissions via Blackboard or in
person per faculty requirement.
