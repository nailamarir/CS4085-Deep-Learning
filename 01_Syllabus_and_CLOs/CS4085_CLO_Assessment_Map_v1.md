# 2. Coverage Analysis

> **These are pedagogical and accreditation-evidence observations. They are NOT changes to the
> syllabus.** The syllabus mapping in Section 1 remains authoritative unless you explicitly
> decide otherwise.

## 2.1 Thinly instrumented outcomes

**CLO6 — single instrument.** Assessed only by the Team Project. If a student's team
underperforms, or if the project grade blends collaboration with technical quality, there is no
independent evidence of the collaboration outcome. ABET 4 evidence rests entirely on one artifact.

*Observation:* The project rubric's five criteria (Technical Implementation, Model Design &
Performance, Innovation & Relevance, Experimental Analysis, Presentation & Report) contain **no
criterion measuring collaboration**. CLO6 maps to the Project, but the Project rubric does not
assess teamwork directly. This is a genuine gap in the accreditation evidence chain.

*If you wish to close it, options include:* a peer-evaluation component within the project grade;
a teamwork criterion added to the rubric; or documented supervision-meeting observations. Any of
these is a change to the syllabus-defined rubric and needs your explicit decision.

**CLO5 — two instruments, no written exam.** Assessed by Lab 5 and the Project only. No midterm
or final question maps to CLO5, so "evaluate deep learning models performance" — arguably the
most transferable skill in the course — has no examination evidence.

## 2.2 Practical outcome assessed on a written exam

**MidtermQ3 and FinalQ3 map to CLO3** ("Build deep learning models using modern frameworks"),
and **FinalQ4 maps to CLO4** ("Implement various deep learning techniques"). These are
build/implement outcomes assessed through exam questions.

The Ministry note describes the Final Exam as "**(Practical and theoretical)**", which suggests a
practical component is expected. The midterm has no such designation.

*Open question Q-009:* Does the midterm include a practical/code component? This affects exam
format, duration, and whether a computer lab must be booked for Week 7.

assessed outcome attached.

*Recorded as Q-007 and Q-010.* Worth confirming with the department, as unattached NQF codes can
surface during accreditation review.

## 2.4 Bloom's taxonomy distribution

Inferred from CLO verbs:

| CLO | Verb | Bloom level |
|---|---|---|
| CLO1 | Demonstrate understanding | **Understand** (L2) |
| CLO2 | Explain | **Understand** (L2) |
| CLO3 | Build | **Apply** (L3) |
| CLO4 | Implement | **Apply** (L3) |
| CLO5 | Evaluate | **Evaluate** (L5) |
| CLO6 | Collaborate | Affective / teamwork |

**Distribution:** 2 × Understand, 2 × Apply, 1 × Evaluate, 1 × affective.

*Observation:* There is **no Analyze (L4) outcome** and no Create (L6) outcome. For a senior-level
(4000) course, the cognitive ceiling sits mostly at Apply. The single Evaluate outcome (CLO5) is
also the most thinly assessed — see §2.1.

In practice the Team Project almost certainly demands Analyze and Create work — the rubric rewards
"Original, well-motivated" solutions and "Thorough analysis, comparisons and critical
interpretation." The *taught and assessed* level is therefore higher than the *stated* CLO level.
This is a CLO-wording matter, not a course-quality matter, but it affects how attainment data
reads to a reviewer.

## 2.5 Balanced elements — no action needed

* **CLO1–CLO4 are well instrumented** across three to five distinct instruments each, spanning
  multiple assessment types. This is solid triangulation.
* **Every assessment type maps to at least one CLO** — no orphaned assessments.
* **Quiz placement is well distributed** (Weeks 3, 5, 9, 11), with each quiz mapping to a
  different CLO in ascending order.
* **Lab progression is coherent:** Labs 1–2 → CLO3 (build), Labs 3–4 → CLO4 (implement),
  Lab 5 → CLO5 (evaluate). Labs escalate through Bloom levels as the semester advances.
